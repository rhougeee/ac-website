What is fiber optic internet

Fiber-optic internet is a high-speed broadband connection that transmits data as pulses of light through ultra-thin strands of glass or plastic.

Benefits of the fiber optic internet

It offers high internet speeds, low latency, reliable connectivity, and better performance for activities such as streaming, online gaming, video conferencing, and downloading large files..

How long does fiber optic installation take?

A standard installation may take a few hours, but complicated installations can take longer depending on the property's layout and the availability of existing fiber infrastructure.

Can fiber optic cables be damaged?

Yes. Excessive bending, crushing, pulling, or improper handling can damage the cable and affect the connection.

Can fiber optic internet support multiple devices?

Yes. It can support smartphones, laptops, smart TVs, security cameras, and other connected devices. Performance depends on your internet plan, router capacity, and simultaneous usage.

How often does fiber optic cabling need replacement?

Fiber optic cables can last for many years when properly installed and protected. Replacement is generally needed only when cables are damaged, degraded, or no longer suitable for the network.

Can fiber optic internet be installed in an apartment or condominium?

Yes, provided that fiber service is available and the building administration or property owner permits the installation.

What equipment is required for fiber optic internet?

Common equipment includes a fiber optic cable, an Optical Network Terminal (ONT) or compatible modem, and a Wi-Fi router. Some devices combine the ONT and router in one unit.

Where should I place my Wi-Fi router at home?

Place it in a central, elevated, and open location away from thick walls, large metal objects, and sources of interference.

How do I maintain my fiber optic connection?

Keep cables protected from bending and physical damage. Avoid disconnecting or touching exposed fiber ends, and contact a qualified technician for repairs.
